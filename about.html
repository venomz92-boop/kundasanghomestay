About Kundasang Homestay

A platform built by locals, for locals — making mountain travel safer, simpler, and more authentic.

Why We Exist

Protecting Travelers in Kundasang

Kundasang is one of Malaysia's most beautiful travel destinations — but it was plagued by homestay scams, fake listings, and unverified hosts taking payments outside any system.

We built Kundasang Homestay to change that. Every property on our platform is physically verified by our local team. Every payment goes through ToyyibPay FPX — Malaysia's trusted online banking gateway. No cash transfers. No scams.

We are not a foreign tech company. We are locals who love Kundasang and want both travelers and hosts to thrive safely.

How We Protect You

Built-In Trust & Safety

🪪
IC Verification
Every host submits a watermarked national ID. Our local team cross-checks identity before any listing goes live.
🏦
ToyyibPay FPX Gateway
All payments route through Malaysia's licensed FPX system. Funds only release to hosts after confirmed check-in.
📅
Real-Time Availability
Live calendar sync prevents double bookings. What you see is always accurate and up to date.
💬
Direct WhatsApp Receipts
After every booking, a pre-filled WhatsApp message with your Booking ID is sent directly to your host for confirmation.
🔐
Encrypted Host Passwords
Host dashboard passwords are SHA-256 hashed with salt and pepper. Raw passwords are never stored.
🛡️
XSS & Injection Protection
Every user input is sanitized using DOMPurify before rendering. No cross-site scripting vulnerabilities.
