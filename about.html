Who We Are

Kundasang Homestay was born out of a simple need & ideas: to connect travelers directly with genuine local hosts in Kundasang, without the hefty fees and most important - scammer. We are a locally run, independent platform built for Malaysians, by Malaysians.

Our mission is to empower homestay owners in the Kundasang area by giving them a direct, transparent, and scam‑free way to welcome guests, while providing travelers with authentic mountain experiences at fair prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

🛡️ Is it safe to book here ? ▾

Yes. All payments are processed via ToyyibPay (a licensed Malaysian payment gateway) with FPX banking. Your money is safe, and we NEVER store any of your details.

✅ How are homestays verified ? ▾

Owners must submit a photo of their IC and bank account. We NEVER store any of your details. Our admin team will reviews and approves every listing before it goes live on listing. We cross‑check everything carefully before approve.

❓ Can I cancel or change dates my booking ? ▾

Of course! Please contact the owner directly or email us at hello@kundasanghomestay.my. Cancellation policies are set by each owner and displayed during booking.

💰 How much does it cost to list my homestay ? ▾

It's FREE to list! We only take a small platform fee when a booking is confirmed, which covers our hosting maintenance, support, and payment processing costs.

📍 What areas do you cover ? ▾

For now we currently only focus on Kundasang, Ranau, and surrounding villages – including Bundu Tuhan, Mesilou, Desa, Kampung Kinasaraban, and more.

📞 How do I contact support ? ▾

You can email us at hello@kundasanghomestay.my or use the contact form below. We typically respond within 24 hours.
