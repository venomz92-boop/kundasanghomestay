Booking Policy

Your Reservation Terms

Last updated: September 2026. Please read these terms carefully before completing your booking.

1. Booking Confirmation

A booking is only confirmed after you have completed payment via our secure FPX gateway (CHIP). You will receive a booking ID and a 6‑digit check‑in code by email. This code must be presented to the host upon arrival. Without the correct code, the host may refuse entry.

2. Payment & Fees

  • Payment: Full payment is required at the time of booking. All payments are processed by CHIP FPX.
  • Service Fee: An 11% service fee plus a fixed RM1.10 gateway fee is added to your total. This fee is non‑refundable except as provided in the Cancellation Policy.
  • Host Payout: Your payment is held securely by CHIP and only released to the host after you have checked in and the host has confirmed your arrival using your 6‑digit code.
  • Currency: All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR).

3. Cancellation & Refunds

Cancellation terms depend on the host's policy and the reason for cancellation. Please refer to our full Cancellation & Refund Policy for detailed rules.

  • Host‑Initiated Cancellation: If a host cancels a confirmed booking, you will receive a full refund (including the service fee). The host may be penalised.
  • Guest‑Initiated Cancellation: You may cancel up to 48 hours before check‑in for a full refund (service fee may be retained). Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to the host’s discretion.
  • Failed Payment: If your payment fails, your booking is void. You may retry after 5 minutes, but availability is not guaranteed.

4. Check‑in & Check‑out

  • Check‑in Time: Typically 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM (local time). Please confirm with the host directly.
  • Check‑out Time: Typically 12:00 PM (noon). Late check‑out may incur extra charges.
  • Check‑in Code: You will receive a unique 6‑digit code via email. You must present this code to the host to complete your check‑in. Without it, the host is not obligated to accommodate you.
  • Guest Identity: The host may verify your identity against the booking details. Please carry a valid ID.

5. Guest Responsibilities

  • Respect the property, its contents, and the surrounding community.
  • Report any damage to the host immediately.
  • Comply with house rules (e.g., noise curfew, smoking policy).
  • Provide accurate contact details so the host can reach you.
  • Obtain travel insurance for your stay.

6. Limitation of Liability

Kundasang Homestay acts as a platform to connect guests and hosts. We are not liable for:

  • Any loss, injury, or damage arising from your stay at a homestay.
  • Disputes between guests and hosts – we will mediate but are not responsible for the outcome.
  • Third‑party services (e.g., CHIP FPX) – we are not liable for payment processing errors.

7. Governing Law

This Booking Policy is governed by the laws of Malaysia. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Sabah.
