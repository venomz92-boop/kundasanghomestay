Cancellation & Refund

Our Refund Rules

Last updated: September 2026. Please read these rules carefully before booking.

1. Guest‑Initiated Cancellation

  • More than 48 hours before check‑in: Full refund of the total amount (including service fee).
  • Within 48 hours of check‑in: No refund, unless the host agrees otherwise. If the host approves, the service fee is non‑refundable.
  • After check‑in: No refund for early departure.

2. Host‑Initiated Cancellation

  • If the host cancels a confirmed booking for any reason, the guest receives a full refund (including the service fee).
  • The host may be penalised (e.g., suspension of listing, placement at the bottom of search results).
  • We will assist the guest in finding alternative accommodation, but we do not guarantee availability.

3. Platform‑Initiated Cancellation (Force Majeure)

In the event of a natural disaster, government travel ban, or other extraordinary circumstances beyond our control, we may cancel bookings and issue a full refund to guests. Hosts will not receive any payout for such cancellations.

4. Payment Failure & Retry

  • If your payment fails, your booking remains in "Pending Payment" status for 15 minutes.
  • You may retry payment after 5 minutes (cooldown imposed by CHIP).
  • If you do not complete payment within 15 minutes, the booking is void and no money is charged.
  • No refund is needed because no payment was taken.

5. Refund Process

  • Refunds are processed via CHIP FPX back to the original payment source (bank account).
  • Refunds typically take 3–7 business days to appear in your account, depending on your bank.
  • If you encounter any issues, contact us at support@kundasanghomestay.my.
