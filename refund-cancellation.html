Cancellation & Refund

Our Refund Rules

Last updated: 16 September 2026. Please read these rules carefully before booking.

📋 Refund at a Glance

There are two possible cancellation paths. Each produces a different refund amount. The host chooses which path when they cancel a booking.

🟢 Host-Initiated Cancellation

The host cancels for their own reasons — overbooking, maintenance, emergency, or any situation not caused by the guest.

You get back
Everything
The full amount you paid — room price, service fee, and gateway fee.
🟡 You Asked to Cancel

You contact the host and ask to cancel. If the host agrees, the cancellation proceeds under this rule.

You get back
Room Price − RM 1.00
The price you paid for the room, minus RM 1.00 to cover the bank's refund processing fee. The service fee and gateway fee are not refunded on this path.

1. Cancelling Your Booking

Guests cannot cancel confirmed bookings directly through the platform. This rule protects hosts from last-minute cancellations that would leave their property empty and unsellable.

If you need to cancel your booking, please follow these steps:

  • Step 1 — Contact your host directly. Reach out through WhatsApp or email using the contact details provided in your booking confirmation email.
  • Step 2 — Request a cancellation. Explain the situation clearly and as early as possible.
  • Step 3 — Await the host's decision. The host will review your request and decide whether to accept.
  • Step 4 — If accepted, the host cancels the booking. A refund is then processed automatically via CHIP.
💰 How Much You Get Back

You get back the price you paid for the room, minus RM 1.00 to cover the bank's refund processing fee. The platform service fee are not refundable on this path, because the cancellation was not caused by the host.

Example: Room price RM 250.00, so you paid RM 278.50 (RM 250.00 room + RM 28.50 service fee). You receive RM 249.00 back — the room price less RM 1.00 for the bank's refund fee. Retained: RM 29.50 (service fee RM 27.50 + gateway fee RM 1.00 + refund fee RM 1.00).

2. Cancellation by the Host

  • If the host cancels a confirmed booking for any reason, you receive a full refund — the room price, the service fee, and the gateway fee. The refund is processed automatically via CHIP.
  • Because you did nothing wrong, the platform absorbs the service fee, the gateway fee, and the bank's refund fee in this case. The host does not receive any payout for a booking they cancelled.
  • The host's listing may be reviewed by our team. Repeated host cancellations may result in suspension of the listing or placement at the bottom of search results.
  • We will assist you in finding alternative accommodation, but we do not guarantee availability.

3. Platform-Initiated Cancellation (Force Majeure)

In the event of a natural disaster, government travel ban, or other extraordinary circumstances beyond our control, we may cancel bookings and issue a full refund to guests. Hosts will not receive any payout for such cancellations.

4. No-Show & Non-Arrival

A "no-show" happens when a guest pays for a booking but never checks in, and never contacts the host or the platform about it.

  • If a guest does not check in on the arrival date and does not contact the host or the platform within 24 hours of the scheduled check-in time, the booking is treated as a no-show.
  • On a no-show, the room was held and could not be resold, so no refund is issued automatically and the host receives the room price in full. The platform retains the service fee and gateway fee.
  • If the guest had a genuine emergency (medical, family, travel ban) and provides evidence, they may write to support@kundasanghomestay.my. Kundasang Homestay reviews these cases — we ask the host for the facts (was the guest in contact; was the night resold) and then apply this policy. The host does not decide the outcome, because the refund is deducted from the host's own payout.
  • On an approved emergency, the guest receives 50% of the room price; the host receives the other 50%; the platform retains the service fee and gateway fee. This is a courtesy, not a right.
ℹ️ Note: If a guest arrives at the property and the listing does not match what was advertised (missing amenities, wrong location, unsafe conditions), that is not a no-show. The guest should contact us immediately with photos. We will investigate and may issue a full refund plus a warning to the host.

5. When a Host Won't Respond

Your cancellation request depends on the host agreeing. We expect hosts to respond within 48 hours. If that doesn't happen — or if you believe the host is being unreasonable — you have a path:

  1. Give the host at least 48 hours to reply to your WhatsApp message or email.
  2. If there is no reply, or the reply is unreasonable, forward the whole conversation to support@kundasanghomestay.my with the subject line "Cancellation Help — Booking [your booking ID]".
  3. Our team will review your case within 3 business days. We may contact both you and the host to understand what happened.
  4. If we decide your request is legitimate and the host has not responded within 48 hours, we will issue the refund ourselves and settle the amount with the host separately. This is called a platform override.
  5. Platform overrides are at our discretion. We will only use them when the guest's request is clearly justified — for example, a family emergency with evidence, or a host who has gone silent for more than 72 hours.
⚠️ Please don't abuse this path. Filing a support ticket doesn't guarantee a refund. Each case is looked at on its own facts. We may ask for evidence (medical certificate, flight cancellation notice, etc.).

6. Payment Failure & Retry

  • If your payment fails, your booking remains in "Pending Payment" status for 15 minutes.
  • You may retry payment after 5 minutes (cooldown imposed by CHIP).
  • If you do not complete payment within 15 minutes, the booking is void and no money is charged.
  • No refund is needed because no payment was taken.

7. How Refunds Are Processed

  • Refunds are processed via CHIP FPX back to the original payment source (your bank account).
  • Refunds typically take 1–7 business days to appear in your account, depending on your bank's processing time.
  • The payment processor (CHIP) charges RM 1.00 for each refund. If you asked to cancel, this RM 1.00 is deducted from your refund. If the host cancelled, the platform pays this RM 1.00 for you.
  • Refunds are processed automatically once the host confirms the cancellation. You do not need to contact CHIP yourself.
  • If you encounter any issues, contact us at support@kundasanghomestay.my.
