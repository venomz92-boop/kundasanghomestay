Kundasang Homestay is a locally-run platform built from the highlands — for travellers who want a real mountain stay, and for the families who open their homes to you.

Every property here is visited in person before it goes online. Every host is someone we've met ourselves. Payments go through CHIP — Malaysia's trusted FPX gateway — and we don't take a single sen in commission from our hosts, so more of every booking stays with the family who owns the home.

We're putting the final touches together now. When we open, we'll announce it here first. If you'd like to be among the first to know — or you just want to say hello — send us a message. We answer every one personally.
