Host Agreement

Partner with Us

Last updated: September 2026. This agreement governs your use of the Kundasang Homestay platform as a host.

1. Introduction

By submitting a listing on Kundasang Homestay, you agree to be bound by this Host Agreement. This is a legally binding contract between you (the host) and Kundasang Homestay ("we", "us", "our").

2. Listing & Verification

  • You must provide accurate, complete, and truthful information about your property, including photos, descriptions, and amenities.
  • You consent to our verification process, which includes submission of a valid national ID (IC) and bank details. We may also conduct a physical inspection.
  • Your IC image and bank QR code are used only for verification and will be deleted from our systems after approval unless you request otherwise.
  • You must keep your listing information (including availability, price, and house rules) up to date.

3. Pricing & Commission

  • You set the nightly price. You receive 100% of the base price per night.
  • We charge no commission to you. Our service fee (11% + RM1.10) is paid by the guest.
  • You may offer discounts or promotions at your own discretion, but you must ensure the displayed price is accurate.

4. Payment & Payout

  • Payments are processed by CHIP FPX. The guest's payment is held in a trust account and released to you only after you confirm the guest's check‑in using their 6‑digit check‑in code.
  • You must have a valid Malaysian bank account in your name. The bank account holder must match your legal name as per your IC.
  • Payouts are normally processed within 1–4 business days after check‑in confirmation, subject to CHIP's processing times.
  • If you fail to confirm check‑in, the payment will remain pending and may be refunded to the guest after a reasonable period.

5. Host Responsibilities

  • Provide a clean, safe, and accurately described accommodation.
  • Honour all confirmed bookings unless exceptional circumstances apply.
  • Communicate promptly with guests, especially regarding check‑in procedures and special requests.
  • Respect the guest's privacy and comply with all applicable laws.
  • Maintain liability insurance for your property (recommended).

6. Cancellations by Host

  • If you cancel a confirmed booking, you must do so through your dashboard and provide a valid reason.
  • We may charge a penalty fee or temporarily suspend your listing if you cancel without justification.
  • The guest will receive a full refund, including the service fee, in the event of a host‑initiated cancellation.

7. Data Protection

We process your personal data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and Malaysian PDPA 2010. You consent to us storing and using your data for the purpose of operating the platform, including verification and payment processing.

8. Termination & Suspension

  • We reserve the right to remove your listing or suspend your account if you violate this Agreement, provide false information, or receive repeated negative guest feedback.
  • You may request to remove your listing at any time by contacting us. Upon removal, your data will be deleted in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Sabah.
