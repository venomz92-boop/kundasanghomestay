Our Policies

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kundasang Homestay.my. By using our platform, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions. If you disagree with any part of these terms, please do not use our website.

Kundasang Homestay connects travellers with verified homestay hosts in Kundasang and Ranau, Sabah. We accept the guest's booking and arrange payment through CHIP (FPX); the accommodation itself is provided by the host.

These Terms should be read together with our Booking Policy, Cancellation & Refund Policy, Payment Disclaimer, Host Agreement and Privacy Policy.

2. Booking & Payments

All bookings made through Kundasang Homestay are processed via CHIP (FPX). Your payment is received by Kundasang Homestay and held until your stay begins. Funds are released to the host within 1–2 business days after guest check-in is confirmed.

💰 0% commission from hosts
Kundasang Homestay does not deduct any commission from the host. The host receives the full room price they set. Our service fee (11% + RM 1.00) is charged to the guest, on top of the room price.
  • Full payment is required at the time of booking
  • Prices are displayed in Malaysian Ringgit (RM)
  • A service fee of 11% plus a RM 1.00 gateway fee is applied to each booking (charged to the guest, added on top of the host's room price)
  • Host payouts are made by bank transfer within 1–2 business days after the guest's check-in is confirmed

The service fee and gateway fee are non-refundable in most cases, with three exceptions: when the host cancels the booking, when the platform cancels under force majeure, or when you cancel the booking yourself at least 14 calendar days before check-in. In those cases the guest receives a full refund including the fees, less the RM 1.00 refund-processing fee in the third case only. For details, see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

⚠️ Payment gateway disclaimer:
Kundasang Homestay is not responsible for payment processing delays, failures, or errors caused by CHIP or the guest's bank. We will assist in resolving issues but accept no financial liability for third-party payment gateway failures. See the Payment Disclaimer.

3. Cancellation Policy

There are several cancellation paths, and which one applies depends on who cancelled the booking and why. Where the guest cancels, the refund is fixed by how far in advance the guest asked, measured from the scheduled check-in time — it never depends on the host's agreement or on how quickly the host replies. The full breakdown is in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

🟢 Host-Initiated Cancellation

If the host cancels for any reason, the guest receives a full refund — room price, service fee, and gateway fee. Kundasang Homestay absorbs all the fees, including the payment provider's refund-processing fee, because the guest did nothing wrong. The refund is initiated automatically. The host's listing may be reviewed by our team, and repeated cancellations may result in suspension.

🟡 Guest-Requested Cancellation

To cancel, the guest emails support@kundasanghomestay.my from the address on their booking, stating clearly that they wish to cancel. The guest needs no reason and needs no host approval; a host cannot refuse, delay, or renegotiate the refund. We acknowledge every request in writing and confirm the exact amount before it is processed. There is no self-service cancellation button, so that no booking is ever cancelled by accident and the amount is always confirmed first.

Refunds for guest-requested cancellations follow three published tiers, measured from the scheduled check-in time (default 2:00 PM MYT on the arrival date) and fixed by the time the guest's message was sent:

A cancellation request must be clear and unconditional. Exploratory or conditional messages do not start the clock, and a refund tier cannot be reserved by asking early and then deciding later — if a booking is reinstated, the clock resets and any later cancellation is recalculated from the new request. A host may voluntarily refund more than these tiers require, but never less.

🔵 Platform-Initiated Cancellation (Force Majeure)

In the event of a natural disaster, a landslide, flood or road closure that makes the property unreachable, a government travel ban or movement restriction, or other extraordinary circumstances beyond anyone's control, we may cancel bookings and issue a full refund to guests — the room price, the service fee, and the gateway fee, with the platform also bearing the RM 1.00 refund-processing fee. Hosts receive no payout for such cancellations, because no stay took place.

Three further outcomes also apply. A no-show — the guest never checks in and never contacts the host or the platform within 24 hours of the scheduled check-in time — results in no refund, with the host paid the room price in full. A guest who provides evidence of a genuine emergency (medical, family, travel ban) may be approved by us for a refund of 50% of the room price, with the other 50% paid to the host; we review these cases ourselves, the host does not decide the outcome, and an approved emergency never stacks on top of a refund tier. If the listing does not match what was advertised, the guest is entitled to a full refund including fees at any time. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

  • All listings must be accurate and up-to-date

  • Properties must be clean, safe, and as described
  • Hosts must verify guest identity upon check-in
  • Hosts must confirm check-in using the guest's 6-digit code on the day of arrival
  • Any issues must be reported to the platform immediately

Full host obligations are set out in the Host Agreement.

5. Guest Responsibilities

  • Guests must respect the property and local community
  • Any damage must be reported to the host immediately
  • Guests are responsible for their own travel insurance
  • Check-in/out times must be respected
  • Guests must present their 6-digit check-in code to the host on arrival

If you are unhappy with a decision we have made under the Cancellation & Refund Policy, you may ask for it to be reviewed by someone else: email us with "Refund Appeal — Booking [your booking ID]" in the subject line and we will reply within 3 business days.
