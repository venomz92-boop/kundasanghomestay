Our Policies

Last updated: August 2026.
Please read these terms carefully before using our platform.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kundasang Homestay. By using our platform, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions. If you disagree with any part of these terms, please do not use our website.

Kundasang Homestay is a platform that connects travelers with verified homestay hosts in Kundasang and Ranau, Sabah. We act as an intermediary to facilitate secure bookings through CHIP FPX.

2. Booking & Payments

All bookings made through Kundasang Homestay are processed via CHIP FPX, Malaysia's trusted online banking gateway. Payments are held securely and only released to hosts after guest check-in is confirmed.

💰 0% Commission – You Keep It All!
Kundasang Homestay does not deduct any commission from the host. The host receives the full listed price. Our service fee (11% + RM1.10) is charged to the guest, not the host.
  • Full payment is required at the time of booking
  • Prices are displayed in Malaysian Ringgit (RM)
  • A service fee of 11% is applied to each booking
  • All payment will directly go to verified host
⚠️ Payment Gateway Disclaimer:
Kundasang Homestay is not responsible for payment processing delays, failures, or errors caused by CHIP or the guest's bank. We will assist in resolving issues but accept no financial liability for third-party payment gateway failures.

3. Cancellation Policy

Cancellation policies vary by homestay. Please review the specific policy on each property listing before booking.

Guests

Please contact the host to confirm your booking before making a payment. In case of an emergency after payment, please reach out to the host directly. All cancellations are subject to the host's policy, not the platform's.

Hosts

Hosts may cancel your booking in the event of an emergency. Please check with the host before making any payment.

4. Host Responsibilities

  • All listings must be accurate and up-to-date
  • Hosts must respond to booking inquiries within 24 hours
  • Properties must be clean, safe, and as described
  • Hosts must verify guest identity upon check-in
  • Any issues must be reported to the platform immediately

5. Guest Responsibilities

  • Guests must provide accurate personal information
  • Guests must respect the property and local community
  • Any damage must be reported to the host immediately
  • Guests are responsible for their own travel insurance
  • Check-in/out times must be respected

6. Privacy & Data Protection

We take your privacy seriously. All personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties except as required to process bookings and payments.
