Privacy Policy

Your Data Matters

Last updated: August 2026. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email, phone number, and identification documents for verification
  • Booking Data: Dates, homestay preferences, payment details
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, and access times
  • Communication: Messages between guests and hosts

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Process bookings and payments via ToyyibPay FPX
  • Verify host and guest identities
  • Communicate booking confirmations and updates
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety
  • Improve our services and user experience

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

🔒
AES-256 Encryption
Data at rest
🔑
SHA-256 Hashing
Passwords + Salt + Pepper
🛡️
DOMPurify
XSS Protection

4. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. Information is shared only:

  • With hosts to complete bookings (name, phone, dates)
  • With ToyyibPay for payment processing
  • With law enforcement when required by law
  • With your explicit consent

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kundasanghomestay.my

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain session state and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work correctly.

🍪 Cookie Notice: We only use essential cookies for functionality. No tracking cookies are used.
