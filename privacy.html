Privacy Policy

Your Data Matters

Last updated: August 2026. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, and identification documents for verification purposes only.
  • Booking Data: Check-in/check-out dates, homestay preferences, payment details, and booking history.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and access times for security and analytics.
  • Communication: Messages exchanged between guests and hosts through our platform.
  • Payment Information: We do not store credit card details. All payments are processed securely through ToyyibPay FPX.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Process bookings and payments via ToyyibPay FPX gateway
  • Verify host and guest identities to prevent fraud or imposter
  • Communicate booking confirmations, reminders, and updates
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes
  • Improve our services and user experience through analytics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

🔒
AES-256 Encryption
All data at rest is encrypted
🔑
SHA-256 Hashing
Passwords with Salt + Pepper
🛡️
DOMPurify
XSS Protection for all inputs

All data transmissions are encrypted using TLS/SSL. We regularly audit our security practices to ensure your information remains safe.

4. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. Information is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With Hosts: To complete bookings, we share guest name, phone number, and booking dates.
  • With ToyyibPay: For payment processing, we share booking amount and transaction details.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law enforcement or regulatory authorities.
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share information.
🔒 Note: We do not share your identification documents (IC) or any sensitive documents with anyone. These are used only for verification purposes.

5. Your Data Rights

Under Malaysian data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we use your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kundasanghomestay.my

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our platform:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality like login and session management.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language and region preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform (anonymized).

You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features.

🍪 Cookie Notice: We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services and fulfill transactions
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations (typically 7 years for financial records)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
  • Improve our services through analytics

You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • ToyyibPay: Payment processing gateway (Malaysia's FPX system)
  • Cloudflare: Security, DDoS protection, and CDN services
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics (no personal data)

These services have their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

📧
Email
support@kundasanghomestay.my

We respond to all privacy inquiries within 48 hours.
