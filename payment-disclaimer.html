Secure Payments via CHIP FPX

Last updated: September 2026. Important information about our payment processor.

1. Third‑Party Payment Gateway

Kundasang Homestay uses CHIP FPX (Financial Process Exchange) to process all online payments. CHIP is a licensed payment gateway in Malaysia, regulated by Bank Negara Malaysia. Your payment data is encrypted and transmitted directly to CHIP; we do not store your banking credentials.

2. Disclaimer of Liability

  • Kundasang Homestay is not responsible for any payment processing errors, delays, or failures caused by CHIP or your bank.
  • We are not liable for any loss or damage arising from your use of the CHIP payment platform.
  • In the event of a disputed transaction, you must contact CHIP directly. We will provide reasonable assistance to resolve the issue.

3. Data Sharing with CHIP

To process your payment, we share the following information with CHIP:

  • Booking ID
  • Guest name and email (for receipt purposes)
  • Booking amount

This data is used solely for the transaction and is subject to CHIP's privacy policy.

4. Acknowledgment

By proceeding with a payment, you agree to the terms of this Disclaimer and consent to the transfer of your necessary data to CHIP for the purpose of completing your booking.
