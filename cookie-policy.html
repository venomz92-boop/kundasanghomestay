Cookie Policy

How We Use Cookies

Last updated: 12 September 2026. This policy explains what cookies are and how we use them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your login state, keep your session secure, and improve your browsing experience.

2. Cookies We Use

Kundasang Homestay uses only two types of cookies:

  • Session Cookies (essential). Two cookies are set when you log in — guest_token for guests and owner_token for hosts. Both are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax, which means JavaScript on the page cannot read them and they are never sent over an unencrypted connection. They exist only to keep you logged in. These cannot be disabled without breaking login.
  • Security Cookies (essential). A CSRF protection token is stored in your browser's localStorage (not as a cookie) to protect forms from cross-site request forgery. It is not used for tracking.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, preference cookies, or any third-party cookies that follow you across other websites.

3. Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate site usage — for example, how many visitors we get per day and which pages are most read. Cloudflare Web Analytics:

  • Does not set any cookies on your device.
  • Does not collect or store personal data.
  • Does not fingerprint your browser or track you across other websites.
  • Reports only aggregate, anonymous statistics to us.

It is a third-party service operated by Cloudflare, Inc., and is used only in this privacy-preserving configuration.

4. Your Control

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies already stored.
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies.
  • Receive a notification when a cookie is set.

Note: Our cookies are session cookies that only exist to keep you logged in. If you disable them, you will not be able to log in to your account, view your bookings, or complete a booking. The public pages of the site will still work.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, contact us at support@kundasanghomestay.my.
